# Rallly brief

## What it's for

Rallly helps people find a date that suits everyone. An organizer suggests a few dates or times, shares one link, and everyone invited marks which ones work for them; the organizer then picks the best one and Rallly sends out the calendar invite.

It replaces the long email or chat thread of "does Tuesday work?" replies. Everyone sees the same table of answers, so the best date is obvious, and nobody needs an account just to answer.

## Who uses it

**Organizers** set up polls for meetings, team events, interviews and social plans. They create a poll, share the link, watch answers come in over a few days, then finalize the date. They can edit, close, reopen, duplicate or delete their own polls, and see who answered what. Organizers with an account see all their polls on the Polls page; teams can share a space.

**Participants** are the people invited to a poll. They open the invite link, mark the dates that work, add their name and, if they like, an email address. They don't need an account. They can see the poll's dates and, unless the organizer hides them, everyone else's answers and comments. They can change their own answers later from the same browser, or from the link in their confirmation email.

## Things it keeps track of

- **Poll**: a question about when to meet. It has a title, an optional location, video call and description, and the suggested dates (options). A poll is **open** while it collects answers, **closed** when the organizer stops it (or once all its dates have passed), and **scheduled** once the organizer finalizes a date. It can also be **canceled**.
- **Option**: one suggested date, or a date and time with a length (30 minutes to 2 hours, or all day).
- **Participant**: someone who answered a poll, with their name and optional email. One participant per person per poll.
- **Vote**: a participant's answer for one option: **Yes**, **If need be** or **No**. An option left unanswered counts as No.
- **Comment**: a note left on a poll, visible to everyone who can see it.
- **Event**: what a poll becomes once finalized: the chosen date, sent as a calendar invite to the people the organizer picks. Events show on the Events page.
- **Space**: where an organizer's polls live. Everyone has a personal space; a team can share a work space with members, billing and branding.

## Words it uses

| Term | Meaning | Not to be confused with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Poll | The list of suggested dates people answer | A survey or vote on anything other than dates |
| Option | One suggested date or time in a poll | Settings, which change how the poll works |
| Participant | Someone who answered a poll | A space member, who shares an organizer's workspace |
| If need be | "I can make it work if I have to" | Yes, which means fully available |
| Finalize | Pick the final date, send the invites and close the poll | Close, which stops answers without picking a date |
| Close | Stop a poll taking new answers without picking a date | Delete, which removes the poll and its answers for good |
| Invite link | The link anyone can use to answer a poll | The edit link a participant gets to change their own answers |
| Space | A workspace for polls; personal or shared with a team | A poll |
| Duration | How long each suggested time lasts, or All day | The poll's deadline |

## Rules that matter

- Deleting a poll deletes its dates, answers and comments, and can't be undone. Close it instead if you might need it again.
- Finalizing closes the poll to new answers and emails a calendar invite to the participants you choose. Only participants who left an email address can be told.
- Removing a date people have voted for deletes their votes for it; Rallly asks first.
- Once anyone has answered If need be, the poll can't be switched to Yes and No only.
- Once votes are in, the poll's time zone is locked.
- Participants can only change their own answers from the browser they answered in, or from the link in their confirmation email. Otherwise the organizer can send them their edit link.
- Anyone with the invite link can answer the poll.

## Rhythm

An organizer creates a poll and shares the link the same day. Answers come in over the next few days; the organizer checks the table and may nudge people or add a date. When enough people have answered, they finalize the date and Rallly emails the invites. Polls whose dates have all passed close on their own.

## Several ways to do one thing

- Share a poll: from the Share window that opens right after creating it (straight away), or from the **Share** button on the poll page (any time later).
- Change your answers: from the menu next to your name on the poll (same browser), or from the link in your confirmation email (any device).

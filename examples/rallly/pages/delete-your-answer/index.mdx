# Remove your answer

Take your name and answers off a poll you've answered. This guide is for people invited to a poll.
{/* aida:end header */}

{/* aida:begin steps-heading */}
## Steps
{/* aida:end steps-heading */}

{/* aida:begin step-open-menu */}
#### 1. Open the menu by your name

Open the poll's link again, in the same browser you answered in. Select the menu next to your name.

*On a computer only.*

![Screenshot of Participants: open the poll's link again, in the same browser you answered in. Select the menu next to your name.](screenshots/step-01.png)
{/* aida:end step-open-menu */}

{/* aida:begin step-open-menu-phone */}
#### 2. Open the menu by your name

Open the poll's link again, in the same browser you answered in. Tap the menu next to your name at the top.

*On a phone only.*

![Phone screenshot of Open the menu by your name: open the poll's link again, in the same browser you answered in. Tap the menu next to your name at the top.](screenshots/phone-step-02.png)
{/* aida:end step-open-menu-phone */}

{/* aida:begin step-choose-delete */}
#### 3. Choose Delete

Select **Delete**.

<DeviceShots devices="desktop,phone">

![Screenshot of Choose Delete: select Delete.](screenshots/step-03.png)

![Phone screenshot of Choose Delete: select Delete.](screenshots/phone-step-03.png)

</DeviceShots>
{/* aida:end step-choose-delete */}

{/* aida:begin step-confirm */}
#### 4. Confirm

Select **Delete** to confirm. Your name and answers are removed; this can't be undone, but you can answer again from the same link with **Add participant**.

<DeviceShots devices="desktop,phone">

![Screenshot of Participants: select Delete to confirm. Your name and answers are removed; this can't be undone, but you can answer again from the same link with Add participant.](screenshots/step-04.png)

![Phone screenshot of Participants: select Delete to confirm. Your name and answers are removed; this can't be undone, but you can answer again from the same link with Add participant.](screenshots/phone-step-04.png)

</DeviceShots>
{/* aida:end step-confirm */}

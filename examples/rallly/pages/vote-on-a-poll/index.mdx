# Say when you're free

Open the invite link, mark the dates that work for you, and add your name. This guide is for people invited to a poll.
{/* aida:end header */}

{/* aida:begin steps-heading */}
## Steps
{/* aida:end steps-heading */}

{/* aida:begin chapter-1 */}
### Part 1: Mark your dates
{/* aida:end chapter-1 */}

{/* aida:begin step-mark-dates */}
#### 1. Mark the dates that work

Open the invite link you were sent. In the **You** row at the top, click each date that works for you. Click once for **Yes**, twice for **If need be** and three times for **No**.

*On a computer only.*

![Screenshot of Participants: open the invite link you were sent. In the You row at the top, click each date that works for you. Click once for Yes, twice for If need be and three times for No.](screenshots/step-01.png)
{/* aida:end step-mark-dates */}

{/* aida:begin step-mark-dates-phone */}
#### 2. Mark the dates that work

Open the invite link you were sent. Under each date, tap **Yes**, **If need be** or **No**.

*On a phone only.*

![Phone screenshot of Mark the dates that work: open the invite link you were sent. Under each date, tap Yes, If need be or No.](screenshots/phone-step-02.png)
{/* aida:end step-mark-dates-phone */}

{/* aida:begin step-continue */}
#### 3. Continue

Select **Continue** below the table.

<DeviceShots devices="desktop,phone">

![Screenshot of New participant: select Continue below the table.](screenshots/step-03.png)

![Phone screenshot of New participant: select Continue below the table.](screenshots/phone-step-03.png)

</DeviceShots>
{/* aida:end step-continue */}

{/* aida:begin chapter-2 */}
### Part 2: Save your answer
{/* aida:end chapter-2 */}

{/* aida:begin step-add-your-name */}
#### 4. Add your name

In **Name**, type your name. Adding your email is optional; it lets Rallly tell you when a date is picked.

<DeviceShots devices="desktop,phone">

![Screenshot of Add your name: in Name, type your name. Adding your email is optional; it lets Rallly tell you when a date is picked.](screenshots/step-04.png)

![Phone screenshot of Add your name: in Name, type your name. Adding your email is optional; it lets Rallly tell you when a date is picked.](screenshots/phone-step-04.png)

</DeviceShots>
{/* aida:end step-add-your-name */}

{/* aida:begin step-save */}
#### 5. Save your availability

Select **Save availability**. Rallly confirms your response has been saved.

<DeviceShots devices="desktop,phone">

![Screenshot of Save your availability: select Save availability. Rallly confirms your response has been saved.](screenshots/step-05.png)

![Phone screenshot of Save your availability: select Save availability. Rallly confirms your response has been saved.](screenshots/phone-step-05.png)

</DeviceShots>
{/* aida:end step-save */}

{/* aida:begin step-see-your-row */}
#### 6. See your answers in the poll

Select **Back to poll**. Your name and answers now show in the poll with everyone else's. To change them later, open the same link again.

*On a computer only.*

![Screenshot of Participants: select Back to poll. Your name and answers now show in the poll with everyone else's. To change them later, open the same link again.](screenshots/step-06.png)
{/* aida:end step-see-your-row */}

{/* aida:begin step-see-your-answers-phone */}
#### 7. See your answers in the poll

Select **Back to poll**. Your name shows at the top of the poll, with your answer beside each date. To change them later, open the same link again.

*On a phone only.*

![Phone screenshot of See your answers in the poll: select Back to poll. Your name shows at the top of the poll, with your answer beside each date. To change them later, open the same link again.](screenshots/phone-step-07.png)
{/* aida:end step-see-your-answers-phone */}

# Leave a comment on a poll

Ask a question or add a note for the organizer and everyone else on the poll. This guide is for people invited to a poll.
{/* aida:end header */}

{/* aida:begin steps-heading */}
## Steps
{/* aida:end steps-heading */}

{/* aida:begin step-open-comments */}
#### 1. Open the comments

On the poll, select **Comments** in the bottom corner.

<DeviceShots devices="desktop,phone">

![Screenshot of 2 Comments: on the poll, select Comments in the bottom corner.](screenshots/step-01.png)

![Phone screenshot of 2 Comments: on the poll, select Comments in the bottom corner.](screenshots/phone-step-01.png)

</DeviceShots>
{/* aida:end step-open-comments */}

{/* aida:begin step-write-comment */}
#### 2. Write your comment

Type your name in **Your name**, then your comment in **Write a comment**. Comments are visible to everyone on the poll.

<DeviceShots devices="desktop,phone">

![Screenshot of Comments: type your name in Your name, then your comment in Write a comment. Comments are visible to everyone on the poll.](screenshots/step-02.png)

![Phone screenshot of Comments: type your name in Your name, then your comment in Write a comment. Comments are visible to everyone on the poll.](screenshots/phone-step-02.png)

</DeviceShots>
{/* aida:end step-write-comment */}

{/* aida:begin step-post-comment */}
#### 3. Add the comment

Select **Add comment**. Your comment shows in the list straight away.

<DeviceShots devices="desktop,phone">

![Screenshot of Comments: select Add comment. Your comment shows in the list straight away.](screenshots/step-03.png)

![Phone screenshot of Comments: select Add comment. Your comment shows in the list straight away.](screenshots/phone-step-03.png)

</DeviceShots>
{/* aida:end step-post-comment */}
